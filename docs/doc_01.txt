This document is synthetic content created for the Zepto GenAI Support Assistant assignment/demo. It is not an official Zepto policy.

Delivery timing:
Orders are delivered within the estimated time shown to the customer at checkout. Actual delivery time can vary because of order volume, traffic, weather, or operational conditions.
Delayed delivery:
If an order is delayed beyond the displayed estimate, the customer should check Order Tracking first. If help is still needed, the customer can contact customer support.
Delivery charges:
Any applicable delivery or handling charges are displayed during checkout before the order is placed.
